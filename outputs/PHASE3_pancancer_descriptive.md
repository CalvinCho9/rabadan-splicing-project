## HLA-A

- 311 total pan-cancer samples across 6 cohorts (no exclusions), 46 candidate AS events.
- **3 (allele, cohort) combination(s) show >=80% penetrance** of that allele's top pattern -- candidate cross-cohort constitutive signal(s), worth a closer look (see the CSV for which).
- **A\*11:01 cross-cohort check:** the BRCA-constitutive pattern `2365_2806->2383_2806` was tested in every pan-cancer cohort carrying A*11:01 (4 cohorts had any carriers at all: the separate NORMAL pool, TCGA-CHOL, TCGA-HNSC, TCGA-LGG -- TCGA-KIRC/UCEC had 0 A*11:01 carriers to test).
  **It recurs at high penetrance in 3 of the 4 cohorts** -- NORMAL pool 20/20 (100%), TCGA-CHOL 3/3 (100%), TCGA-LGG 6/7 (85.7%) -- consistent with a constitutive, allele-intrinsic isoform rather than a BRCA- or even tumor-specific effect. TCGA-HNSC is the outlier at 1/3 (33.3%), but n=3 there is too small to weigh against the other three cohorts. Pooled across all pan-cancer cohorts: 30/33 (91%) A*11:01 carriers show the pattern. This is a genuinely useful generalization of Finding 3 from `ANNOTATION_ARTIFACT_NOTE.md` -- still descriptive (no denominator here is a proper matched-normal comparison), but it strengthens the case that this is a real, fixed minor isoform of the A*11:01 reference transcript and not a BRCA-cohort artifact.
